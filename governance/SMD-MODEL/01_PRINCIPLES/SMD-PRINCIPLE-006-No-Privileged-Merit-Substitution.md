# No Privileged Merit Substitution

Privilege, lineage, or capital cannot substitute evidence-based merit.

## Key Elements

- Merit cannot be inherited, purchased, or assigned by patronage.
- Anti-clientelism enforcement (see `SMD-GOV-005`).
- Evidence is the only currency of recognition.
