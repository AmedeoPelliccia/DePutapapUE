# Dignity Floor

Establishes the non-negotiable dignity baseline for every person affected by the corporation/cooperation.

## Key Elements

- No programme, contract, or efficiency goal may breach the dignity floor.
- Applies to contributors, users, neighbouring communities, and ecosystems.
- Enforced as a non-derogation clause.
