# 00_CONTROL — Control Section

## 1. Purpose

The `00_CONTROL` section governs the control layer of the **Social-Merito-Democratic Corporation / Cooperation Model**.

It defines the status, scope, vocabulary, applicability, and change-control logic required to keep the `SMD-MODEL` baseline consistent, traceable, and auditable.

This section does not define programme execution content. Instead, it establishes the governance controls that validate programme, technology, infrastructure, organizational, and neural-network records under the SMD model.

---

## 2. Scope

This section applies to all controlled artefacts located under:

```text
governance/SMD-MODEL/

```

It directly controls:

* Document status and lifecycle tracking.
* Controlled vocabulary and definitions.
* Applicability rules across entities.
* Change-control logic and authorizations.
* Baseline integrity and versioning.
* Traceability to lifecycle governance.
* Linkages to merit-evidence and democratic review records.

## 4. Control Principles

* **Baseline Control:** The SMD model shall remain strictly versioned, reviewable, and traceable throughout its lifecycle.
* **Single Source of Truth:** Each controlled rule or definition shall reside in exactly one authoritative location.
* **Controlled Vocabulary:** Core terms—such as *merit*, *dignity*, *accountability*, *programme*, *technology*, *infrastructure*, and *neural-network system*—shall be used with absolute consistency across all domains.
* **Applicability Discipline:** Each artefact shall explicitly state whether it applies to the entire model, a specific operational axis, or a localized programme implementation.
* **Change Traceability:** Every controlled modification shall be fully justified, peer-reviewed, authorized, and cryptographically or systematically recorded.
* **Evidence Linkage:** All governance claims shall structurally link to corresponding verification records where applicable.
