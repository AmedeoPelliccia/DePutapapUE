# SMD-MODEL 080 — Mandatory Inheritance and Rights Protection

Defines inherited rights and dignity protections that apply across all SMD layers and programmes.

## Key Elements

- Inheritance of the dignity floor by all programmes.
- Non-derogation clauses.
- Rights protection for contributors, beneficiaries, and affected communities.
