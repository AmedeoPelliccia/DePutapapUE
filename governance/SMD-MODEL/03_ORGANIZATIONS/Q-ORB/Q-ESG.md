# Q-ESG — Environmental, Social and Governance

## 1. Purpose

`Q-ESG` governs environmental, social, and governance accountability within the Social-Merito-Democratic model.

It ensures that programmes, technologies, infrastructures, and neural-network systems are evaluated not only by delivery performance, but also by environmental impact, social accountability, governance integrity, and regenerative value.

---

## 2. ESG Scope

| Axis | Scope |
|---|---|
| Environmental | Emissions, energy, materials, circularity, pollution, lifecycle footprint, and regenerative impact. |
| Social | Human dignity, worker rights, DEI, accessibility, community impact, safety, and anti-abuse safeguards. |
| Governance | Decision traceability, authority records, auditability, risk control, ethics, compliance, and democratic accountability. |

---

## 3. Controlled Rule

```yaml
governance_rule:
  id: SMD-Q-ESG-001
  title: "No Regenerative Claim Without Evidence"
  status: mandatory
  rule: >
    No programme, technology, infrastructure, or neural-network system shall
    claim ESG alignment unless environmental, social, and governance evidence
    is traceable, reviewable, and lifecycle-linked.
```

---

## 4. Q-ESG / Q-DEI Interface

Q-DEI is part of the social and governance dimensions of Q-ESG, but remains a separate Q-ORB function because representation, equity, accessibility, and anti-discrimination require dedicated authority.

- Q-ESG = environmental + social + governance accountability
- Q-DEI = diversity + equity + inclusion + representation safeguards

---

## 5. Controlled Closure Statement

Q-ESG converts sustainability and social responsibility into auditable governance.

It prevents vague ESG claims by requiring lifecycle evidence, measurable indicators, accountable ownership, and democratic review.
