# Q-ORB — Enterprise Support and Governance Functions

Q-ORB groups the enterprise support, governance, accountability, and assurance functions.

## Key Elements

- `Q-FINANCE` — Financial planning, controlling, treasury, and capital governance.
- `Q-HR` — People, dignity floor, fair access, and skills.
- `Q-CSR` — Corporate and social responsibility; community and ecological accountability.
- `Q-DEI` — Diversity, equity, inclusion, accessibility, anti-discrimination, and representation safeguards.
- `Q-ESG` — Environmental, social, and governance accountability and sustainability reporting.
- `Q-LEGAL` — Legal, regulatory, and contracts.
- `Q-PMO` — Programme management office and portfolio governance.
- `Q-RISK` — Enterprise risk, safety, and assurance.
- `Q-GOV` — Governance, ethics, audit, and democratic-review secretariat.
