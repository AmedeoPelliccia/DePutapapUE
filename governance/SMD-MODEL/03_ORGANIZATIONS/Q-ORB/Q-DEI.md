# Q-DEI — Diversity, Equity and Inclusion

## 1. Purpose

`Q-DEI` governs diversity, equity, inclusion, accessibility, anti-discrimination, representation, and dignity safeguards within the Social-Merito-Democratic model.

It ensures that merit is evaluated under fair access conditions and that management structures do not reproduce inherited privilege, exclusion, identity conformity, tokenism, informal patronage, or hidden power capture.

---

## 2. Controlled Definition

`Q-DEI` means:

```text
Diversity, Equity and Inclusion
```

Within the SMD-MODEL, `Q-DEI` is a Q-ORB governance function, not a symbolic communication layer.

It controls:

* representation objectives;
* anti-discrimination safeguards;
* accessibility requirements;
* fair-access conditions;
* dignity protections;
* anti-tokenism controls;
* demographic monitoring rules;
* privacy and self-identification safeguards;
* DEI evidence requirements;
* interface rules with Q-HR, Q-LEGAL, Q-GOV, Q-CSR, and Q-ESG.

---

## 3. Representation Objective

Q-DEI maintains a controlled representation objective for management and decision-making bodies.

```yaml
management_representation_objective:
  status: controlled_target
  scope:
    - management_bodies
    - leadership_pipelines
    - committees
    - boards
    - review_panels
    - authority_structures
  target_distribution:
    women: 0.40
    men: 0.38
    queer_and_other_underrepresented_groups: 0.22
  interpretation: >
    Representation objective for management bodies, leadership pipelines,
    committees, boards, review panels, and authority structures.
    This objective is intended to counter structural exclusion and improve
    democratic legitimacy, not to replace competence, role suitability,
    accountability, or evidence-based merit.
```

This is a **governance target**, not an automatic appointment rule.

Selection shall remain based on:

* competence;
* role suitability;
* evidence-based merit;
* ethical conduct;
* accountability;
* fair access;
* safety culture;
* lifecycle responsibility.

## 4. Legal and Ethical Safeguards

```yaml
safeguards:
  no_forced_disclosure: true
  self_identification_only: true
  privacy_protected: true
  aggregate_reporting_only: true
  no_exclusionary_hiring: true
  no_identity_tokenism: true
  merit_required: true
  accessibility_required: true
  legal_review_required: true
  anti_discrimination_required: true
  jurisdiction_specific_compliance_required: true
```

Q-DEI shall not require any person to disclose protected characteristics.

All representation metrics shall use lawful, voluntary, privacy-protected, aggregated, and non-punitive data.

Individual identity data shall not be used for coercion, retaliation, surveillance, forced classification, or exclusionary decision-making.

---

## 5. Controlled Rule

```yaml
governance_rule:
  id: SMD-Q-DEI-001
  title: "Representation Without Tokenism"
  status: mandatory
  rule: >
    Management representation shall be actively monitored and corrected
    where structural exclusion is detected, but no appointment shall be made
    without competence, accountability, role suitability, and evidence-based merit.
```

---

## 6. Merit Boundary

Q-DEI exists to prevent the false use of merit as inherited privilege.

The following are prohibited substitutes for merit:

```yaml
prohibited_merit_substitutes:
  - inherited_privilege
  - social_proximity
  - informal_patronage
  - obedience_without_reason
  - identity_conformity
  - political_loyalty
  - opaque_managerial_preference
  - unreviewable_discretion
```

Valid merit shall be based on:

```yaml
valid_merit_basis:
  - competence
  - contribution
  - responsibility
  - reliability
  - ethical_conduct
  - uncertainty_reduction
  - role_suitability
  - lifecycle_traceability
  - evidence_based_performance
```

## 7. Q-DEI Operating Responsibilities

| Responsibility              | Description                                                                                      |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Representation monitoring   | Monitor management and decision-body representation using lawful, voluntary, aggregated data.    |
| Fair-access review          | Identify structural barriers to entry, promotion, training, recognition, and authority.          |
| Anti-discrimination control | Detect and escalate discriminatory practices, exclusion patterns, harassment, or retaliation.    |
| Accessibility governance    | Ensure that participation, training, review, and promotion mechanisms are accessible.            |
| Anti-tokenism review        | Prevent symbolic appointments without authority, support, competence pathway, or decision power. |
| Merit-system audit          | Verify that merit criteria are evidence-based and not proxies for inherited privilege.           |
| Privacy protection          | Ensure no forced disclosure or misuse of protected identity data.                                |
| Governance reporting        | Provide DEI evidence to Q-ESG, Q-GOV, Q-HR, and Q-LEGAL where applicable.                        |

---

## 8. Boundary Statement

Q-DEI does not replace Q-HR, Q-LEGAL, Q-GOV, Q-CSR, or Q-ESG.

It interfaces with them:

| Interface | Role                                                                                                 |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Q-HR`    | Hiring, promotion, training, competence, wellbeing, and workforce data.                              |
| `Q-LEGAL` | Labour law, anti-discrimination law, privacy, equality law, and compliance review.                   |
| `Q-GOV`   | Decision records, governance traceability, voting, authority records, and review logs.               |
| `Q-CSR`   | Social responsibility, stakeholder dignity, community impact, and ethical commitments.               |
| `Q-ESG`   | ESG reporting, social metrics, governance indicators, and sustainability accountability.             |
| `Q-RISK`  | Risk identification, DEI-related nonconformance, systemic exclusion, and corrective action tracking. |

---

## 9. Evidence Requirements

Q-DEI claims shall be backed by evidence.

| Evidence Class | Description                                                                     |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `DEI-REP`      | Aggregated representation metrics and trend evidence.                           |
| `DEI-ACCESS`   | Fair-access, training, promotion, and opportunity evidence.                     |
| `DEI-RISK`     | Exclusion, discrimination, harassment, retaliation, and tokenism risk evidence. |
| `DEI-REVIEW`   | Review-panel, appeal, grievance, and correction evidence.                       |
| `DEI-PRIVACY`  | Self-identification, data minimization, and privacy-safeguard evidence.         |
| `DEI-LEGAL`    | Jurisdiction-specific legal review and compliance evidence.                     |

---

## 10. Review and Escalation

Q-DEI shall escalate to the appropriate authority when it detects:

* forced disclosure of protected characteristics;
* discriminatory selection or promotion patterns;
* tokenistic appointments without real authority;
* structural exclusion from leadership pipelines;
* retaliation against DEI-related complaints;
* lack of accessibility in governance or training;
* misuse of identity data;
* governance bodies with persistent uncorrected imbalance.

Escalation interfaces:

| Issue                                     | Primary Escalation |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Labour or anti-discrimination law         | `Q-LEGAL`          |
| Hiring, promotion, competence pipeline    | `Q-HR`             |
| Governance decision traceability          | `Q-GOV`            |
| ESG reporting and external accountability | `Q-ESG`            |
| Abuse, retaliation, or systemic risk      | `Q-RISK`           |

---

## 11. Controlled Closure Statement

Q-DEI protects diversity, equity, inclusion, accessibility, and dignity while preserving competence, accountability, safety culture, and evidence-based contribution.

It shall not be used to force disclosure, create tokenistic representation, bypass merit, or impose unlawful exclusion.

Its purpose is to ensure that merit operates under fair conditions and that management authority reflects democratic, inclusive, and reviewable governance.
