# Q-Divisions — Technical and Scientific Capability Domains

Q-Divisions are the technical and scientific centers of excellence of the SMD corporation/cooperation.

## Key Elements

- `Q-AIR` — Aeronautical engineering, aerodynamics, propulsion integration, and flight sciences.
- `Q-SPACE` — Space systems engineering, orbital mechanics, and mission assurance.
- `Q-HPC` — High-performance and quantum-classical computing.
- `Q-GREENTECH` — Green technologies, hydrogen, and sustainable energy systems.
- `Q-STRUCTURES` — Primary, secondary, and cryogenic structures; materials and qualification.
- `Q-DATAGOV` — Data governance, semantics, synthetic data, and AI governance.
- `Q-GROUND` — Ground segment, MRO, airports, vertiports, and ground robotics.
- `Q-MECHANICS` — Mechanical systems, mechanisms, actuation, and tribology.
- `Q-INDUSTRY` — Industrial systems, FALs, manufacturing, and supply-chain integration.
- `Q-HORIZON` — Long-horizon research, beyond-2040 concepts, and exploratory science.
