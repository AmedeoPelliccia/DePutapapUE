# SMD-MODEL 040 — Social-Merit-Democratic Governance

Defines the integrated governance model combining social dignity, evidence-based merit, and democratic accountability.

## Key Elements

- Dignity floor as non-negotiable baseline.
- Merit measured by evidence, not by privilege.
- Democratic review with quorum and appeal.
